Getting WHOLE into AI answers

How WHOLE shows up inside ChatGPT and Claude, and how it becomes the source AI answers cite for every health and beauty trend it covers. Fibermaxxing is the test case.

Recommendation

Run two tracks. Start now: publish a crawlable daily report that AI answers can cite. Start in November: put WHOLE inside ChatGPT and Claude as an app. Both tracks use the same content, and that content leads with what only Quilt has: first-hand data on what people are saying, checked against published research.

What this tells us

1

WHOLE can't outrank hospitals and journals on the health question.

Medical and government sources back 25 of 30 answers, across all four categories. WHOLE shouldn't compete for that slot.

2

The "what people are saying" slot is open on every trend.

Every prompt asked what people are saying. The answers built that section from news stories and Reddit threads. Perplexity said outright, on 4 of its 14 answers, that its read was "not a representative poll." Measured trend data showed up once in 30 answers: Circana, for fibermaxxing. Quilt's Discourse and Trends pipelines measure exactly this, and Circana's one citation shows research firms can get cited.

3

Both assistants already answer in WHOLE's format.

Almost every answer I checked opened with a verdict ("mostly hype," "real, but oversold"). People clearly ask questions shaped like WHOLE's Real or hype section. WHOLE needs to be the source behind those answers.

4

AI crawlers can't read WHOLE yet.

The live site loads its content with JavaScript, so a crawler sees only the menu labels. It also has no robots.txt or sitemap. All of the issue content and the 16 verified Real or hype verdicts are hidden from AI crawlers today.

The two tracks

Track A · start now

Be the source AI cites

  • The WHOLE Daily: one short public page a day, with one trend number from an Anthropologic run and one Real or hype verdict with its studies. Each page is dated and has a permanent link.
  • A topic page for every trend WHOLE covers, across Nutrition, Beauty, Exercise and Sleep (fibermaxxing, creatine for women, mouth taping, beef tallow and more), updated weekly: what's being posted vs searched, the verdict, who's talking, what's next.
  • A format that gets cited: the answer in the first two lines, plus statistics, quotes and citations. Those three changes raised a source's visibility in AI answers by up to 40% in Princeton's GEO study.
  • Plain pages AI crawlers can read: static HTML, a sitemap, schema markup, and access for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. Submit the sitemap to Bing and Google Search Console.
  • Written by the agents we already have: the Editor Agent writes from pipeline output, and the Evidence Agent checks each verdict.
Track B · November

Be inside the assistant

  • Precedent: Sephora launched an app inside ChatGPT on Mar 24, 2026 (US pilot). It offers catalog Q&A and links to a Beauty Insider account. Checkout isn't available yet.
  • One server, two stores: ChatGPT apps and Claude connectors both run on MCP. ChatGPT has accepted app submissions since Dec 2025, and Claude's connector directory lists 950+ servers.
  • Three actions: real_or_hype(claim), whats_people_saying(topic), shop_the_trend(topic). Purchases link out to retailers, with affiliate disclosure.
  • The pitch: Sephora's app answers from its own catalog. WHOLE is the neutral alternative, with products ranked by how much people talk about them, not by commission.
  • Head start: the verified verdicts and the search agent already exist.

Proposed MVP for end of October

  • Crawlable public pages: topic pages for the 16 trends that already have verified verdicts, and the first daily reports, plus robots.txt, a sitemap and schema markup.
  • Public pages show only real data. The prototype's issue numbers are placeholders. Until we have API access, the 16 verified verdicts are the only content that can go live, so pipeline access is needed right after we agree on the strategy.
  • A baseline prompt set covering every topic, not just fibermaxxing: about 20 prompts run weekly in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews, logging whether WHOLE is cited.
  • The app prototype stays as the product demo. Track B starts in November.
